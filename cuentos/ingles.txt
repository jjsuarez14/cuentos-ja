=== en-01 | 0 | jue | noche | en | Little Bear Can Try | "Todavía no puedo… ¡pero lo intento!" | Cuento original en inglés

This is Little Bear. Little Bear is brown. Little Bear is small. Little Bear lives in a big green forest with Mama Bear.

One morning, Little Bear sees a tall tree. At the top of the tree, there is a red apple. A big, red, shiny apple.

"I want that apple!" says Little Bear.

Little Bear jumps. Jump! But the apple is too high.

"I can't do it," says Little Bear.

Mama Bear smiles. "Not yet," she says. "You can't do it yet. But you can try again!"

Little Bear jumps again. Jump, jump! Still too high.

"I can't do it… yet!" says Little Bear. "I will try again!"

Little Bear finds a rock. He stands on the rock. He jumps. Jump, jump, jump!

He touches the apple! But the apple does not fall.

"I can't do it… yet!" says Little Bear. "I will try again!"

Little Bear thinks. He thinks and thinks. Then he has an idea.

"Mama, can you help me?"

"Yes, I can help you," says Mama Bear.

Mama Bear lifts Little Bear up, up, up. Little Bear stretches his arm. He stretches and stretches and…

Got it! The red apple is in his paw!

"I did it!" says Little Bear. "I tried and I tried, and I did it!"

"Yes, you did it," says Mama Bear. "And you asked for help. That is very smart."

Little Bear and Mama Bear sit under the tree. They share the red apple. Crunch, crunch, crunch. It is sweet and yummy.

The sun goes down. The sky is orange, then pink, then blue. The stars come out, one by one.

Little Bear yawns. "Mama, tomorrow I want to climb the tree by myself."

"Can you do it?" asks Mama Bear.

Little Bear smiles. "Not yet… but I will try!"

Mama Bear gives him a big bear hug. "Good night, Little Bear."

"Good night, Mama."

Little Bear closes his eyes. And he dreams of red apples and tall, tall trees.

The end.

@@ Para papá
try /trái/ — intentar · "I will try again!" = "¡Lo intentaré otra vez!"
yet /iét/ — todavía · "Not yet" = "Todavía no"
jump /yamp/ — saltar
high /jái/ — alto (la "h" suena como una j suave)
help /jelp/ — ayudar · "Can you help me?" = "¿Me ayudas?"
yawn /ion/ — bostezar
Consejo: que J.A. repita con usted la frase "I can't do it… yet! I will try again!" cada vez que aparece. Lea despacio y señale con el dedo, como si saltara.

=== en-02 | 0 | mar | noche | en | Max Shares His Bananas | Compartir | Cuento original en inglés

This is Max. Max is a little monkey. Max lives in a tall tree in the jungle.

Max loves bananas. Yellow bananas. Sweet bananas. Big bananas.

One day, Max finds a bunch of bananas. One, two, three, four, five bananas!

"Mine!" says Max. "All mine!"

Max sits on a branch. He eats one banana. Yum!

Then Max sees his friend Parrot. Parrot is red and green.

"Hello, Max," says Parrot. "I am hungry."

Max looks at his bananas. He has four bananas.

"Mine," says Max. "All mine."

Parrot flies away. Parrot is sad.

Then Max sees his friend Turtle. Turtle is slow and green.

"Hello, Max," says Turtle. "I am hungry."

Max looks at his bananas. He has four bananas.

"Mine," says Max. "All mine."

Turtle walks away. Slowly, slowly. Turtle is sad.

Max eats another banana. Yum. But now… Max is not happy. He is alone. Nobody is playing with him. Nobody is talking with him.

"Hmm," says Max. "Bananas are good. But friends are better."

Max climbs down the tree. He runs to Parrot. "Parrot! Here is a banana for you!"

"Thank you, Max!" says Parrot. Parrot is happy.

Max runs to Turtle. "Turtle! Here is a banana for you!"

"Thank you, Max!" says Turtle. Turtle is happy.

Now Max has one banana. Parrot has one banana. Turtle has one banana.

They sit together under the big tree. They eat their bananas. Yum, yum, yum!

Parrot sings a song. Turtle tells a story. Max laughs and laughs.

"Sharing is fun!" says Max.

"Sharing is fun!" says Parrot.

"Sharing… is… fun!" says Turtle, very slowly.

The moon comes up. The jungle is quiet. Max climbs into his tree. Parrot flies to her nest. Turtle hides in his shell.

"Good night, friends," says Max.

"Good night, Max."

And Max falls asleep, with a happy heart and a full tummy.

The end.

@@ Para papá
share /sher/ — compartir · "Sharing is fun!" = "¡Compartir es divertido!"
mine /máin/ — mío · "All mine" = "Todo mío"
hungry /jángri/ — con hambre
friend /frend/ — amigo
together /tuguéder/ — juntos
thank you /zénk iu/ — gracias (la "th" con la lengua entre los dientes)
Consejo: haga voces distintas para cada animal. El loro rápido y agudo, la tortuga muuuy lenta. A J.A. le va a encantar repetir "Mine! All mine!" y luego "Sharing is fun!".

=== en-03 | 0 | jue | noche | en | Ten Little Stars | Calma para dormir | Cuento original en inglés

The sun goes down. The sky is dark blue. It is time to sleep.

But wait! Look up! One little star.

One little star says, "Hello! Good night!"

Look again. Two little stars. Twinkle, twinkle.

Two little stars say, "Hello! Good night!"

Look again. Three little stars. Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle.

Three little stars say, "Hello! Good night!"

Four little stars. Over the house.

Five little stars. Over the tree.

Six little stars. Over the park.

Seven little stars. Over the sea.

The little stars are happy. They dance in the sky. Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle.

A little owl looks up. "Hoo, hoo! Hello, stars!"

A little frog looks up. "Ribbit, ribbit! Hello, stars!"

A little boy looks up from his window. He has curly hair. He has a big orange ball. He waves his hand.

"Hello, stars!" says the little boy.

And the stars say, "Hello, little boy! Are you ready for bed?"

"Almost," says the little boy. "First, I want to count you."

The moon comes out. The moon is big. The moon is round. The moon is white.

The moon says, "Hello, little stars. It is time to shine."

Eight little stars. Over the mountain.

Nine little stars. Over the city.

Ten little stars. Over my window.

Ten little stars. Twinkle, twinkle, twinkle.

The cat is sleeping. Shhh.

The dog is sleeping. Shhh.

The birds are sleeping in the tree. Shhh.

The fish are sleeping in the sea. Shhh.

Mama says, "Good night."

Papa says, "Good night."

And the ten little stars say, "Good night, little one. We will watch over you."

Now close your eyes.

Count with me, very slowly.

One… two… three… four… five…

Six… seven… eight… nine… ten…

Ten little stars, shining bright.

Good night, stars. Good night, moon.

Good night, sweet dreams.

Shhh… sleep now.

The end.

@@ Para papá
star /star/ — estrella · "little star" = "estrellita"
twinkle /tuínkel/ — titilar, brillar
moon /mun/ — luna
shine /sháin/ — brillar
sleep /slip/ — dormir · "sweet dreams" /suít drims/ = "dulces sueños"
Números: one /uan/, two /tu/, three /zri/, four /for/, five /fáiv/, six /siks/, seven /séven/, eight /éit/, nine /náin/, ten /ten/.
Consejo: este cuento se lee cada vez más lento y más bajito. Cuenten juntos con los dedos. Es un cuento muy corto a propósito: si J.A. lo pide, léalo dos veces seguidas.

=== en-04 | 0 | mar | noche | en | The Big Orange Ball | Practicar hasta que salga | Cuento original en inglés

This is a ball. A big, orange ball. It is a basketball.

The ball goes bounce. Bounce, bounce, bounce!

This is Sam. Sam is a little boy. Sam loves the big orange ball.

Sam goes to the park with Papa. In the park, there is a basket. A tall, tall basket.

"Papa, I want to shoot!" says Sam.

Sam holds the ball. He looks at the basket. He throws the ball. Up, up, up!

Oh no! The ball does not go in. The ball falls down. Bounce, bounce, bounce.

"I can't do it," says Sam.

"Not yet," says Papa. "Try again!"

Sam bends his knees. He throws the ball. Up, up, up!

Oh no! The ball hits the basket. Bonk! It falls down. Bounce, bounce, bounce.

"I can't do it… yet!" says Sam. "I will try again!"

Papa smiles. "Look at the basket. Bend your knees. And… jump!"

Sam looks at the basket. Sam bends his knees. Sam jumps and throws the ball. Up, up, up!

Swish! The ball goes in the basket!

"I did it! I did it!" says Sam. He jumps and jumps.

"Yes, you did it!" says Papa. "High five!"

Sam and Papa do a high five. Clap!

"Again! Again!" says Sam.

Sam throws the ball. Swish! Sam throws the ball. Bonk! Sam throws the ball. Swish!

Some balls go in. Some balls do not go in. But Sam is happy. Sam keeps trying.

The sun goes down. It is time to go home.

Sam holds the big orange ball. He is tired. Papa carries him on his shoulders.

At home, Sam takes a bath. Splash, splash. He puts on his pajamas. He brushes his teeth. Brush, brush.

Sam gets in bed with his big orange ball.

"Good night, ball," says Sam.

The ball does not say anything. Balls can't talk. But Sam thinks the ball is smiling.

"Good night, Sam," says Papa.

Sam closes his eyes. He dreams of baskets. Swish, swish, swish.

The end.

@@ Para papá
ball /bol/ — pelota · bounce /báuns/ — botar, rebotar
basket /básket/ — canasta · shoot /shut/ — tirar a la canasta
throw /zróu/ — lanzar
knees /niis/ — rodillas · "bend your knees" = "dobla las rodillas"
High five! /jái fáiv/ — ¡choca esos cinco!
Consejo: haga los sonidos con ganas (bounce, bonk, swish!) y, al terminar, choque los cinco con J.A. diciendo "High five!".

=== en-05 | 0 | jue | noche | en | The Magic Words | Portarse bien: por favor y gracias | Cuento original en inglés

This is Lily the Bunny. Lily is white and soft. Lily has long ears.

Lily is going to the market with her friends.

At the market, there is a Fox. The Fox sells carrots. Orange carrots. Crunchy carrots.

Lily runs to the Fox. "Carrot! Give me a carrot!"

The Fox looks at Lily. "Hmm," says the Fox. "What is the magic word?"

Lily does not know. "Magic word? What magic word?"

Her friend Duck says, "Please! The magic word is please!"

Lily smiles. "A carrot, please."

"Of course!" says the Fox. And he gives Lily a big orange carrot.

Lily runs away with her carrot. Crunch, crunch!

"Wait, Lily!" says Duck. "There is another magic word!"

Lily stops. "Another one?"

"Yes!" says Duck. "Thank you!"

Lily runs back to the Fox. "Thank you, Mr. Fox!"

The Fox smiles a big smile. "You are welcome, little Bunny!"

Then Lily sees Bear. Bear has a red ball.

Lily wants to play. She says, "Bear, can I play with your ball, please?"

"Yes!" says Bear. "Let's play together!"

Lily and Bear play with the ball. They throw and catch, throw and catch.

Then, Lily bumps into Duck. Bump! Duck falls down.

Oh no! Lily helps Duck stand up. "I'm sorry, Duck."

"That's okay," says Duck. "Thank you for saying sorry."

"Wow," says Lily. "Please. Thank you. I'm sorry. These are magic words!"

"Yes," says Duck. "They make everybody happy."

At night, Lily goes home. Mama Bunny gives her a cup of warm milk.

Lily says, "Thank you, Mama."

Mama Bunny smiles. "You are welcome, my little bunny."

Lily gets in bed. "Mama, can you read me a story, please?"

"Of course," says Mama Bunny.

Mama reads a story. Lily's eyes close. Slowly, slowly.

"Good night, Lily."

"Good night, Mama. Thank you."

The end.

@@ Para papá
please /pliis/ — por favor
thank you /zénk iu/ — gracias · "You are welcome" /iu ar uélcam/ = "de nada"
I'm sorry /aim sóri/ — lo siento, perdón
magic words /máyic uords/ — palabras mágicas
together /tuguéder/ — juntos
Consejo: después de leerlo, juegue a usar las palabras mágicas durante el día: "Water, please!", "Thank you!". Celebre cada vez que J.A. las diga, en inglés o en español.
